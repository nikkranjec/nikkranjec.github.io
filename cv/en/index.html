I’m interested in people. How we think, what shapes us, what we care about and what brings us together.

It started in 2014 on a road bike. I mounted a camera on it because after a race I wanted to see how it had really played out, then edited the footage and posted it on YouTube. My cycling career ended later, but the filming stayed. The same curiosity led me to study electrical engineering, because I wanted to know how things work, and I taught myself the creative skills along the way.

I’m interested in people. How we think, what shapes us, what we care about and what brings us together. So I gradually added marketing to video production, branding to marketing, and now I’m going one step further, into community building.

But who I am is perhaps best told by the videos I make just for myself.

When I join a project, I first look at what already exists. Often, the answer is already there, in the name, in the character of the product or in the people behind it. It just needs to be brought together into a story that makes sense to the people it’s meant for.

People don’t respond to what you show them, but to the part of themselves they recognise in it. So I look for the story there, in how they live, what drives them and what matters to them. Once I understand that, I can take the work from the initial idea and strategy all the way through filming, editing and final delivery.

PaintCam

Branding and strategy, practical effects, camera assistant, 3D animation, VFX, social media content, project management

Main adKickstarter

Who’s on guard when you’re not home?

PaintCam, 3D animation, 2024

When our team came on board, PaintCam, a security camera that can mark an intruder with paintballs, was still a prototype made of 3D-printed parts, and the team behind it was all engineers. Before we filmed anything, we decided who the campaign was for. The product’s original market was in Africa, but Kickstarter is backed by people who can afford to risk money on something that might not work out, so we aimed at the US.

The product looked like EVE from the film WALL-E, so we gave it a similarly calm, confident and slightly playful character, carried by the question of who’s on guard when you’re not home. We built the brand, the strategy and the Kickstarter page and shot the main ad, and in Blender I made a 3D animation that showed the product without the prototype’s flaws. The campaign raised almost $80,000 on Kickstarter, the launch was picked up by international tech media, and it was also mentioned on the WAN Show of the Linus Tech Tips channel.

2023

Il Bilaterale

Branding, strategy, filming and editing

First campaign video

When our team came on board, the restaurant was only just taking shape. The owners already knew what it would be called, what would be on the menu and how they wanted to work, and we tied all of that into a story about a Slovenian owner working across the border, in Italy. Hence the name Il Bilaterale, meaning “the bilateral”, two-sided.

The same duality went into the content, as we shot the videos in Slovenian and Italian, 20 per shooting day. We put a waiter who felt at home there in front of the camera, and the campaign turned him into one more reason to visit, as guests also came to say hello to him in person.

2020

Kranjska Gora

Co-organising and content creation

At Kranjska Gora I was one of the organisers and at the same time one of the content creators, so in those days I slept two to three hours a night and spent the rest of the time filming or editing.

In 2020, Kranjska Gora became the first Slovenian tourist destination to open its own TikTok channel. The parents of today’s teenagers knew it as a luxury winter resort from Yugoslav times, while younger people had no real feeling for it, so as a team we first wrote it a new story, and then shot 100 videos in five days with thirteen content creators.

Under #kranjskagori the videos gathered 4 million views, twice the target, and the channel grew from zero to 5,000 followers in its first month. For me it was the real start of my creative path, as before that I worked more as a hobbyist, and then everything began to open up.
