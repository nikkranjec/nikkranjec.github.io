THE LAST STEP
One intention, for all six days
An intention is not a goal. Nothing gets measured and nothing can fail. It is one line about how you want to meet these six days, and it works quietly: it sits at the top of every morning, and when the island offers more than you can hold, it helps you feel which yes is yours.
Write it the way you speak. Begin with I, keep it small enough to be true, and let it be about you rather than what the week should deliver.
